B-Side legal

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 August 2026

1. Who is responsible for your data?

The B-Side project team operates this educational service and determines how personal data is used within it. You can contact the team, exercise a privacy right, or report a concern using the contact form in the footer of the home page.

2. Information we process

Account and profile information

  • user ID, username, display name, email address, role, avatar, and account status;
  • a salted password hash for local accounts—we do not store the original password; and
  • Google profile information returned through OAuth, such as name, email, and profile picture, when you choose Google sign-in.

Music and library information

  • uploaded audio, cover images, artist and album information, titles, genres, and technical metadata;
  • playlists, likes, recent plays, listening duration, skips, completions, and replays; and
  • audio characteristics produced by the analysis service, including tempo, mood, energy, danceability, and related feature vectors.

Social and communication information

  • friend requests, friendship status, online presence, conversations, messages, read or delivery status, and shared songs;
  • artist applications and moderation decisions; and
  • your name, email, message, and delivery metadata when you use the contact form.

Technical information

The service necessarily handles request information such as IP address, request time, browser headers, authentication status, and error information to deliver requests, apply rate limits, diagnose failures, and protect the platform. Not all of this information is stored permanently.

3. Why we use this information

  • to create accounts, authenticate users, and provide profiles;
  • to store, validate, analyze, stream, organize, and remove music and images;
  • to provide playlists, likes, history, statistics, chat, friendships, and online status;
  • to calculate personalized music recommendations from your interactions and audio features;
  • to process artist applications, administer roles, enforce rules, and respond to messages;
  • to prevent abuse, enforce rate limits, maintain security, and troubleshoot the service; and
  • to meet legal obligations and respond to valid legal requests.

Depending on the context, these uses are based on providing the service you request, our legitimate interest in operating and securing an educational platform, your consent where required, or compliance with a legal obligation.

4. Recommendation profiling

B-Side assigns weights to actions such as likes, plays, completions, skips, and replays. It combines those signals into a preference vector and compares it with machine-extracted audio features to rank recommendations. This profiling affects which music suggestions you see. It does not make decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about you.

5. Where data is stored and who receives it

Account, catalog, social, and activity data is stored in the project's PostgreSQL database. Audio and images are stored in the project's S3-compatible MinIO storage. The separate audio analysis service reads uploaded audio to calculate features and returns those features to the main backend.

Data may also be handled by:

  • Google when you choose Google OAuth, under Google's own privacy terms;
  • the configured email provider when you submit the contact form; and
  • hosting or infrastructure providers used by the team to run the project.

We do not sell personal information. Administrators and moderators receive only the access necessary for their role. Other users see profile, friendship, message, artist, playlist, or catalog information only as required by the relevant feature.

6. International transfers

Third-party services such as Google or an email provider may process information outside your country. Their safeguards and transfer mechanisms are described in their own privacy notices. The self-hosted B-Side database, object storage, and analysis services communicate within the project's deployment environment.

7. Retention

Account and profile data is generally kept while the account remains active. Uploaded content, playlists, likes, messages, friendships, and activity history are retained while needed to provide those features or until the associated account or content is deleted. Contact requests are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to respond and maintain an appropriate record. Security information may be retained as needed to investigate abuse and protect the service.

Because B-Side is an educational project, the deployment or its data may also be removed when the project ends. Users should not treat B-Side as permanent storage.

8. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask to:

  • access and receive a copy of your personal data;
  • correct inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • delete your account or personal data;
  • restrict or object to certain processing;
  • receive portable data where applicable; or
  • withdraw consent without affecting processing already performed.

Profile information can be corrected in account settings. For access, deletion, portability, objection, or other requests, use the home-page contact form and identify the account concerned. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request. You may also complain to your local supervisory authority; in France, this is the CNIL.

9. Browser storage and third-party cookies

B-Side stores the authentication token in your browser's local storage so you remain signed in. Logging out removes that token. Google may use its own cookies or browser storage during OAuth; those technologies are controlled by Google. B-Side does not use advertising cookies or sell data for targeted advertising.

10. Security

B-Side uses HTTPS for browser connections, salted Argon2 password hashing, authenticated API routes, role-based permissions, upload validation, private audio storage with time-limited URLs, and container-network isolation. No service is perfectly secure, so you should use a unique password and avoid sharing sensitive information in messages.

11. Children

B-Side is not directed to children under 16. If you believe a child has provided personal data without the consent required by law, contact the team so it can be reviewed and removed where appropriate.

12. Policy updates

This Policy may be updated when B-Side's features, infrastructure, or legal obligations change. The effective date will be revised, and material changes will be communicated reasonably.

13. Contact

Send privacy questions and requests to the B-Side project team through the contact form in the footer of the home page.
